Sky imagery in Little Seadom

The interactive viewer renders procedural volumetric clouds and atmospheric scattering.

Earlier versions of the viewer and previously rendered media used a reframed
and color-graded hemisphere from the following HDR panorama.
Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky), Greg Zaal and Jarod Guest, Poly Haven.
https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky
CC0 1.0.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The previously rendered film and stills also use Cumulus Clouds on Horizon,
Defi22 (2017). That photograph was reprojected, cropped, color graded and
blended with the spherical survey above. The earlier composite panorama is
